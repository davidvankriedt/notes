One's expectations of outcomes, fearful or eager, tarnish the inevitable process of an event's realisation. I am, regardless of any strong refutations, in constant fear of failure - there exists a space where I attempt and fail, and still benefit, but I am yet to feel comfortable in it. In practice, an idea forms, plays out in the imagination, and falls on either side. But in doing so, I fail to let it fall elsewhere. In many occasions - autobiographies, documentaries - I have witnessed the power of the story - its indefinite resolution - and it has always been expectations that have deceived me; promises that one starts with, linger on noisily, but in the absence of them, a space opens up for curiosity. A substance that is endless. Curiosity has enough weight to kill expectations, no matter how scary they may seem.
