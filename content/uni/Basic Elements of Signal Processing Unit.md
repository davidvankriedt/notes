
Signal Processing - manipulation of signal to get desired shaping, transforming a signal from time domain to frequency, vice versa, smoothening the signal, separating the noise from signal.

Signals that exist in nature are continuous signals. Continuous-time (analog) signals exist for the continuous interval (t1, t2), which can range from -infinity to infinity.
