A __security engineer__ solves risks in digital and physical systems. It's about designing systems that remain safe when humans, data, software, and AI are under attack. 

#### Why?
- Fix early, save cost.
- integrated thinking - embeds security into every phase.
- results in more robust and trustworthy systems for users.

#### What do security engineers do?
- design secure systems, not just secure code
- anticipate attacker behaviour, including AI-assisted attacks
- protect data, identity, and user trust
- evaluate trade-offs between security, usability, cost, and privacy
- prepare systems for failure, abuse, and recovery
- think about human behaviour, not only technical controls

#### Lifecycle
1. identify assets
2. assess threats & risks
3. design controls
4. implement solutions
5. test & audit
6. monitor & update continuously

#### Is having open-source code safe?

__Risks__:
- public exploits
- supply chain risks (Typosquatting packages)
- lack of maintenance
- abandoned packages
- dependency confusion
- compromised build pipelines
- AI-generated insecure code copied into projects

How do we ensure that open-source projects remain secure and trustworthy?


The roles of attackers and defenders are symmetrical.

#### Foundational Components of Security: The CIA(NA) Triad 
- __Confidentiality__ - ensuring that information is only accessible to those authorised to see it.
- __Integrity__ - ensuring data is accurate and hasn't been tampered with.
- __Availability__ - Ensuring system, data and functionalities are accessible when needed.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- __Non-repudiation__: Guaranteeing that a sender cannot deny the authenticity of their signature on a document or a message they originated.
- __Authentication__: Verifying the identity of users and systems.


#### Why is Data/Information so important to industry?

__Data helps organistations__:
- train AI models
- personalise services
- detect fraud
- understand users
- make decisions

__Data also creates risk__:
- privacy breaches
- identify theft
- profiling and manipulation
- deepfake personalisation
- targeted phishing
- discrimination and bias
- legal and reputational damage
