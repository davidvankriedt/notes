Introduction to modern cyber security design, practice, and regulation. Concentrating on __analytical skills__, an engineering approach to security design, an understanding of the __offensive mindset__, and understanding of the role of law and other forms of regulation, and an interpretation of __criminological implications__ of cybersecurity issues.
