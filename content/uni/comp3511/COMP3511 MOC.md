# User Interface Design

Aims to teach the process of use-centred design including methods and techniques, providing background knowledge about how people think and process information.

## Main aims:
- User interface and visual design principles.
- To teach you different data collection methods from users and how to analyse the collected data.
- To give an understanding of how to design & develop paper-based and electronic-based prototypes of UIs.
- To provide experience in the formal evaluation of use interfaces for their usability.
