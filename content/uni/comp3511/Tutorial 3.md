Usability goals vs user experience goals

Usability Goals - can people get things done? (objective, measurable)
User experience goals - how does it feel to use? (subjective)


## Proximity - group with space
Things placed together seem related. The opposite is true.

## Contrast - build a logical path
size, colour, shape, all give different impressions of different elements in the page

## Balance - symmetry & asymmetry
Balanced page feels stable, and unbalanced one feels like it's tipping over.

A page can be both asymmetrical and balanced if there is equal weight on both sides (words vs images).

# The Standard

## WCAG & the POUR principles
The Web Content Accessibility uidelines are the international standard, Everything rests on four principles remember them as POUR:
- Perceivable - can people sense the content (alt text for images, captions for video)
- Operable - users can operate it (keyboard works, enough time, no seizure risks).
- Understandable
- Robust
